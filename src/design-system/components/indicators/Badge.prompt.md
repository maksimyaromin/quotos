Use `Badge` for a short status word or a limit-window scope tag ("Opus", "Weekly"). Sentence-case, muted tones.

```jsx
<Badge tone="warn">Behind</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">Opus 4</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral` (scope tags), `accent` (teal), `warn` (amber), `danger` (red), `info` (slate-blue). Keep the label to one or two words.
